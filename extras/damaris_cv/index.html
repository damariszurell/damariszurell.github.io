Damaris Zurell - Curriculum vitae

Nov 27, 2026

Contact - Academic appointments - Education - Scientific leadership - Research funding - Honours and awards - Academic service - Scientific meetings - Memberships

I am Professor of Ecology and Macroecology at the University of Potsdam. My research focuses on the mechanisms and predictability of biodiversity change across biological, spatial and temporal scales, linking individual-level ecological processes to population and community dynamics and large-scale biodiversity patterns.

My research group integrates ecological theory, biodiversity observations and quantitative models to understand biodiversity dynamics and anticipate ecological futures under global environmental change. Alongside our fundamental ecological research, we advance the conceptual and methodological foundations of biodiversity modelling, including modelling frameworks, data–model integration and international modelling standards.

I serve as Editor-in-Chief of Ecography and contribute to the coordination of international biodiversity modelling initiatives and interdisciplinary research on global change ecology.
